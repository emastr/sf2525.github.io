About the Conference

Welcome to the Poster Session for SF2525! This year, we have a diverse lineupp of 17 interesting projects. Throughout the day, you will take turns giving talks, attending and presenting at two scientific poster sessions. Before lunch, we will have a vote and announce the best poster. The session will be held in Q21, Malvinas Väg, on May 18th from 08:00 to 12:00. I encourage you to engage with the presenters, ask questions, and enjoy the variety of topics covered in stochastic differential equations. This session serves as a simulation of a real scientific conference and is usually how networking and collaborations start, so make the most of it! Bonus points if you can find common projects and discuss similarities and differences in approaches.
