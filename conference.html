General Information

Welcome to the Poster Session for SF2525! This year, we have a diverse lineup of 18 interesting projects. Throughout the day, you will take turns giving talks, attending and presenting at two scientific poster sessions. Before lunch, we will have a vote and announce the best poster. The session will be held in Q21, Malvinas Väg, on May 18th from 08:00 to 12:00. I encourage you to engage with the presenters, ask questions, and enjoy! This session serves as a simulation of a real scientific conference and is similar to how networking and collaborations start. Bonus points if you can find common projects and discuss similarities and differences in approaches.
